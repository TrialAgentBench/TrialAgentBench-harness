# One argument, three reader tasks

Home answers "so what": an analysis can pass every surface check and still drive the wrong development
decision. It states what the benchmark measures and why, then what scores do not measure. Methods and evidence lets a reviewer judge whether a score
means what it claims; the release evidence sits directly after the construct-validity argument whose
evidence column it supplies. Run it starts an operator. Once model results are published, Results
compares the evaluated systems.

Every sentence comes from the harness documents or the release evidence record. The site adds headings,
navigation and table captions only, so the documents stay the single source of the argument and a change
to them reaches the site on the next build.

Use a portable Arial/Liberation Sans family across prose and figures. Figures are shown at no more than
their designed size; type is never inflated to fill spare space. Tables, figures and commands scroll inside
keyboard-reachable regions on compact screens, where a figure keeps a legible minimum width rather than
shrinking its type. Every page reads completely without JavaScript and in
print.
