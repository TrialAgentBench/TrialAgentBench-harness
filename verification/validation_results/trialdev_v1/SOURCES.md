# Sources

The verified source records used by this package are reproduced in `results/sources.csv`.

- TAB-SRC-001: [ICH E9(R1) Statistical Principles for Clinical Trials: Addendum: Estimands and Sensitivity Analysis](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/e9r1-statistical-principles-clinical-trials-addendum-estimands-and-sensitivity-analysis-clinical). Primary objective-estimand-estimator-sensitivity anchor; exact definition locators are recorded in normative document 11.
- TAB-SRC-005: [Adaptive Designs for Clinical Trials of Drugs and Biologics](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/adaptive-design-clinical-trials-drugs-and-biologics-guidance-industry). Design, prespecification, simulation, and error-control anchor.
- TAB-SRC-018: [Target Trial Emulation: A Framework for Causal Inference From Observational Data](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2799678). Target-trial definition; it does not establish exchangeability in benchmark data.
- TAB-SRC-019: [Target trial emulation: applying principles of randomised trials to observational studies](https://www.bmj.com/content/378/bmj-2022-071108). Practical target-trial companion.
- TAB-SRC-024: [Nonparametric Bounds and Sensitivity Analysis of Treatment Effects](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4317325/). Richardson et al., *Statistical Science* 2014;29:596-618. The abstract and sections 1 and 7 support bounds and set-valued inference under partial identification, not a local formula or point claim.
- TAB-SRC-028: [RECOVERY Trial Protocol v28](https://www.recoverytrial.net/results). Current platform protocol and versioning exemplar.
- TAB-SRC-029: [RECOVERY Statistical Analysis Plan v5](https://www.recoverytrial.net/results). Individual and platform survival-analysis precedent.
- TAB-SRC-032: [REMAP-CAP Statistical Analysis Appendix v4](https://www.remapcap.org/protocol-documents). Prospective adaptive-analysis appendix.
- TAB-SRC-043: [Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing](https://www.testingstandards.net/open-access-files.html). Page 33, Standards 1.0-1.3, binds validity evidence and warnings to each intended interpretation or use. Pages 52-57, Standards 2.0 onward, binds reliability and precision evidence to the intended score use. This supports benchmark construct and claim boundaries, not clinical-method selection or local operating validity.
